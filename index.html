Our Story

Founded in 2009, Golden Nuts began as a small family business with a passion for sharing the authentic flavors of Middle Eastern cuisine. What started in our grandmother's kitchen has become a beloved destination for premium Arab desserts, dried fruits, and handcrafted chocolates.

Every product we offer tells a story of tradition, craft, and love. From sun-dried dates of the Jordan Valley to hand-made baklava using centuries-old techniques, we bring you the best of Arab culinary heritage.
